Straight talk

Which one are you?

Nearly everyone who messages us is one of these three. Most people are two.

  • You didn't choose it

    “I'm still useful. I just don't have a job any more.”

    Then run something. We need people who know how to make a Saturday actually happen — budgets, rosters, the lot. Nobody here will ask you to sit quietly at the back.

  • You earned it

    “Forty years I waited for this. Don't waste my time.”

    Fair. Come for the good parts only. No committee, no attendance list, no obligation to come back next week if it wasn't for you.

  • Nobody warned you

    “Monday came and I had nowhere to be.”

    Start with the Saturday walk. By next week you'll have somewhere to be and people expecting you — which turns out to be the whole trick.

Our purpose

NextGen neighbours, living with purpose through mutual support, close to home.

There are more of us at this stage of life than ever, and the support built for it sits in too many separate places. Community Unlimited puts it back where it belongs — in the neighbourhood, run by the people who live there.

Connect & contribute

Bringing people with experience and energy together to create events and shared experiences of their own.

Purpose after retirement

Challenging the idea that this stage is about winding down. Asking for help is normal here — and so is giving it.

One-stop simplicity

One community-based way in, instead of a maze of schemes, forms and phone numbers.

Inclusive by design

Adaptable support for everyone — from the tech-savvy to those who'd rather just be told in person.

Who it's for

Built by NextGen, open to everyone around them

Experienced. Active. Curious. Still contributing. Not defined by age — defined by what comes next.

  • If this is you

    NextGen

    People with experience, energy and a little time to spare — looking for purpose, company, and a way to keep contributing.

  • If this is your parent

    Families & care networks

    Sons, daughters, and the wider network of people helping day to day.

  • If you want to back it

    Stakeholders & partners

    Grassroots and government bodies, care providers, and corporate or philanthropic partners.

How it works

Five steps from joining to leading

Everyone starts as a neighbour. Where you go after that is up to you — most people stop at step three, and that's a good place to stop.

  1. Step 01

    Register

    Message us on WhatsApp. We'll ask your name, your block, and what you enjoy.

  2. Step 02

    Train

    Short, practical sessions run by neighbours who already do the thing.

  3. Step 03

    Qualify

    A friendly sign-off so everyone knows you're ready. No exams.

  4. Step 04

    Deploy

    Take real shifts at real events, at a pace that suits your week.

  5. Step 05

    Lead

    Run your own session and bring the next group of neighbours through.
