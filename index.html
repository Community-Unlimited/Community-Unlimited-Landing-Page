Our purpose

Seniors ageing with purpose, through mutual support in their own neighbourhood.

Singapore's population is ageing, and support for it sits in too many separate places. Community Unlimited puts it back where it belongs — in the neighbourhood, run by the people who live there.

  • 1

    Connect & contribute

    Bringing healthy, active seniors together to create events and shared experiences of their own.

  • 2

    Purpose after retirement

    Challenging the stigma around ageing. Asking for help is normal here — and so is giving it.

  • 3

    One-stop simplicity

    One community-based way in, instead of a maze of schemes, forms and phone numbers.

  • 4

    Inclusive by design

    Adaptable support for every senior — from the tech-savvy to those who'd rather just be told in person.

How it works

Five steps from joining to leading

Everyone starts as a neighbour. Where you go after that is up to you — most people stop at step three, and that's a good place to stop.

  1. STEP 01

    Register

    Message us on WhatsApp. We'll ask your name, your block, and what you enjoy.

  2. STEP 02

    Train

    Short, practical sessions run by neighbours who already do the thing.

  3. STEP 03

    Qualify

    A friendly sign-off so everyone knows you're ready. No exams.

  4. STEP 04

    Deploy

    Take real shifts at real events, at a pace that suits your week.

  5. STEP 05

    Lead

    Run your own session and bring the next group of neighbours through.

Who it's for

Built with seniors, open to everyone around them

  • Primary

    Seniors

    Active adults aged 60–70 seeking purpose, connection, and a way to keep contributing.

  • Secondary

    Families & care networks

    Children of ageing parents and the wider network of people helping them day to day.

  • Tertiary

    Stakeholders & partners

    Grassroots and government bodies, care providers, and corporate or philanthropic partners.
